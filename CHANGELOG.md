# Changelog

Every version that reaches the Chrome Web Store gets an entry here. The version in `package.json` is the only one to edit;
the build copies it into the extension's manifest.

## 0.1.0 (not released yet)

- Syncs your CSFloat trade history through the tab you're logged in to, and pairs each sale with its buy.
- Flips, Holds and Other folders, plus your own. Move rows by menu, right-click or drag.
- Sales with no buy go to Other on their own. Folder rules send new buys of a type to a folder.
- Needs review for sales that could match more than one buy.
- Compact and Detailed views. Detailed shows item images, stickers, charms, fade, and float against the skin's float cap.
- Adjusted float, as trade-ups use it.
- Analytics: net profit over time, by month and by item type, with best and worst flips.
- Export to .xlsx with working formulas.
